Rethinking Resilience, Ambition, and Community

Who We Are

Downshift is a nonprofit, member-supported project dedicated to rethinking resilience, ambition, and community in the 21st century.

Through coaching, conversation, and cultural programming, we create space for reflection, mutual support, and more sustainable ways of living. Membership helps sustain this work and allows it to remain accessible to a wide range of participants.
